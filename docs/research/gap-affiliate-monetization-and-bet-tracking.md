# Affiliate Monetisation & Bet Tracking for an Irish/UK Horse-Racing Tips App

**Research date: 28 August 2026.** All program statuses and terms below were checked live on this date unless otherwise noted. Anything that could not be verified from a primary or dated secondary source is explicitly flagged.

> **Method note / limitation.** General web search was unavailable for most of this session; findings rest on direct fetches of official affiliate-program pages and T&Cs, the Betfair developer documentation (offline PDF dated 22 Aug 2025), Zendesk/Confluence APIs, Google News RSS (for dated headlines), and reachable secondary sources. Several UK/IE gambling sites geo-block non-UK/IE traffic (Tote consumer site, BetVictor, Racing Post main site, betdaq.com, developer.betfair.com), which limited some checks; workarounds are noted inline.

---

## Key takeaways

- **The single most important finding: Betfair closed its UK & Ireland affiliate programme in May 2025.** New onboarding stopped immediately and existing affiliates were phased out ("all links deactivated and commissions halted by the end of the transition window") — reported by SBC News (26 May 2025), Yogonet (28 May 2025) and Affiverse (29 May 2025). The plan's assumed "exchange affiliate" route via Betfair is gone for UK/IE traffic.
- **Flutter's UKI brands are effectively out of the open affiliate channel**: Paddy Power's affiliate domain (paddypoweraffiliates.com) is a parked page; Sky Bet ended its affiliate programme in Sep 2017 ("as regulatory pressure mounts" — Racing Post, 4 Sep 2017); Betfair UK&I closed May 2025. Large media partners (e.g. myracing) still carry live Betfair tracking links (`bfpartners.click`), implying bespoke media deals continue — but that door is not open to a small new site.
- **Programs verified open to new affiliates for UK (and mostly IE) racing traffic**: bet365 Partners, Entain Partners (Ladbrokes/Coral), BoyleSports Affiliates (30% sports revshare, lifetime), UK Tote Affiliates (RavenTrack; UK+IE only; **no negative carryover**; 3-year revshare window), Matchbook Affiliates (lifetime share of exchange commission), William Hill Affiliates, 888/evoke (888sport), FDJ United Affiliates (Unibet — UK listed, **Ireland not listed**).
- **Negative carryover is the norm at bookmakers** (bet365, Entain, BoyleSports carry negative months forward against future commission; Matchbook discretionary), with one notable exception: **Tote Affiliates resets negative months to zero**. For a value-bet product likely to refer winning bettors, this difference is existential.
- **Every audited program has an activity quota** that a small site can fail: bet365 requires 15 active customers in 3 months (account closure otherwise); Tote can terminate below 10 new customers/month; Matchbook cuts revshare to 5% if <6 new depositors in 6 months; BoyleSports cuts the rate 5pts/month (max −10pts) below 1 referral/month; Entain voids balances under £100 accrued over 12 months.
- **The tipster/affiliate conflict is structural and documented**: revenue-share affiliates "will be paid a monthly commission when referred players collectively lose money" and earn "in the region of 10–40% of the net revenue" (Punter2Pro, updated 21 Jul 2026). Sky Bet's 2017 exit followed press scrutiny of tipsters profiting from followers' losses (Guardian, 4 Sep 2017). Exchange programs (only Matchbook remains open) structurally avoid this because commission is charged to winners too.
- **Bet tracking with user consent is officially supported on Betfair Exchange**: the Vendor Web API provides an OAuth2 flow (identitysso.betfair.com/view/vendor-login) for licensed software vendors to "carry out operations on the users behalf", and `listCurrentOrders`/`listClearedOrders` return the user's open and settled bets. A personal live App Key costs a one-off **£299**; the delayed key is free. No equivalent consumer API exists at any Irish/UK bookmaker — comparable products either place the bet themselves (Racing Post app with William Hill, Ladbrokes, Coral, Paddy Power in-app accounts) or use manual entry.
- **No published per-player revenue/EPC figures for UK/IE racing affiliates could be verified.** The best verifiable anchors: Entain pays "€6+ million every month" across "10,000+" affiliates (≈€600/affiliate/month mean, heavily skewed); Flutter sold Oddschecker for £155m (July 2021); 888 reports $207.9m paid to affiliates lifetime; Punter2Pro's 10–40% NGR revshare band.

## 3. The sharp-traffic problem

**The structural conflict is contractual, not anecdotal.** Under every bookmaker revshare contract audited, commission = % of *net losses* of referred players. Punter2Pro states it plainly: a bookmaker affiliate "will be paid a monthly commission when referred players collectively lose money" and "Bookmakers — and by extension, Revenue Share affiliates — only earn when their players lose" (https://punter2pro.com/twitter-tipsters-bookmaker-affiliates/, updated 21 Jul 2026). For a *value-betting* product whose promise is that users beat the market, a revshare book is a machine for accumulating negative balances: at bet365, Entain and BoyleSports those negative balances **carry forward** and swallow future commission; only the Tote resets them monthly.

**Documented industry reactions:**
- **Sky Bet closed its affiliate programme on ~1 Sep 2017** — "Sky Bet end affiliate programme as regulatory pressure mounts" (Racing Post, 4 Sep 2017); the same week The Guardian ran "Pressure grows on gambling industry to stop tipsters profiting from losses" (4 Sep 2017), which framed tipster-affiliates earning a share of followers' losses as the scandal. (Headlines and dates verified via Google News RSS; full article text could not be retrieved this session — both outlets block the research proxy.)
- **Betfair's UK&I closure (May 2025)** was attributed by Affiverse to regulatory pressure and "reduced ROI from affiliate channels… falling lifetime value" — consistent with operators cutting the channel when economics or compliance sour, at zero notice to affiliates. Contractually, every audited program lets the operator terminate at will and stop paying post-termination (e.g. Matchbook: "You will not be entitled to referral fees occurring after the date of termination"; Betfair legacy: "no revenue share applies post-termination").
- **Clawback powers are standard**: Matchbook "retain[s] the right to retract the commissions paid to you at any time" for traffic it deems non-genuine; BoyleSports retains commission when referred customers fail affordability/source-of-funds checks; Entain sets off "amounts already received by you… generated by any Prohibited Activity" and bans "rake-back activity"; bet365 deducts commission "paid in error… or if a Customer does not meet the qualification or earning requirements". None of the audited T&Cs contains an explicit "we may terminate you because your players win" clause — operators do not need one, because (a) winners simply generate no commission under revshare, and (b) every contract permits no-fault termination and unilateral commission changes (Entain: 15 days' notice).
- **Individual termination anecdotes** (affiliate accounts closed after referring winning/arbing players) circulate on affiliate forums (GPWA) and matched-betting communities, but **could not be verified directly this session** — gpwa.org and the relevant forums were unreachable through the research proxy. Treat specific horror stories as unverified; treat the contractual asymmetry above as fully verified.

**How the incumbent tips/odds sites live with it:**
- **OLBG** (olbg.com/about-us): pure affiliate-media model — "If you click through to any of the betting sites or casino sites listed on this site then OLBG may receive a payment" — across a huge *recreational* audience (500,000 members joined over the years; 1.1m+ app installs; £1m+ tipster prize money; ~800 monthly prizes). Scale and audience mix mean the portfolio of referred players loses in aggregate even if star tipsters win; OLBG monetises the crowd, not the sharp minority.
- **Oddschecker / FairPlay Sports Media**: Flutter sold Oddschecker Global Media to Bruin Capital for **£155m** (£135m cash + £20m deferred), announced 13 Jul 2021 (Casino Guardian). Now rebranded **FairPlay Sports Media** (fairplaysportsmedia.com; oddscheckerglobalmedia.com 301s there), it sells operators "flexible revenue share models", runs the **Confido Network** (operator-publisher affiliate network) and a BetTech odds API spanning "200+ sportsbooks", claiming 100m+ monthly uniques. At that scale the relationship inverts: bookmakers pay for distribution/media (tenancy, CPA at volume), not marginal player-loss share — insulation a solo app will not have.
- **myracing** (Spotlight Sports Group — Racing Post's owner): free racing tips explicitly funded by affiliate/free-bet placement; on 28 Aug 2026 its pages carried live tracked links to bet365 (`bet365.com/hub/aff/open-account-horse-racing?affiliate=365_04014021`), William Hill (`campaigns.williamhill.com/C.ashx?btag=a_197196b_788c_&affid=1680471&siteid=197196…`), Tote (`trk.tote.co.uk/o/WkP8Ai?s1=myracing`), Betfair (`bfpartners.click/o/4cgkM5?...site_id=102372`), BoyleSports (`ads.boylesports.com/redirect.aspx?pid=51651&bid=7584`) and kwiff (`trk.eggaffiliates.com`). Note the model: myracing's tips skew toward mass-market acquisition (sign-up offers front and centre), not sharp value-betting.
- **Exchange deals as the escape hatch**: on an exchange the operator's take is commission on net *winnings* per market — winners generate revenue, so an affiliate share of exchange commission (Matchbook's "Net Betting Commission… for the lifetime of that customer") does **not** collapse when referred players win. The catch, per Punter2Pro: "there's little incentive to promote betting exchanges over traditional bookmakers" because absolute payouts are far smaller (2–4% commission on net winnings vs 10–40% of gross losses). With Betfair UK&I closed, Betdaq gone and Smarkets discontinued, **Matchbook is the only open exchange affiliate program** for UK/IE racing traffic.

## 4. Deep-linking mechanics (one-click to bookmaker/betslip)

- **Betfair (legacy but documented)**: any Betfair URL becomes an affiliate deep link by appending `?AFF_ID=<your id>` (partnerships.betfair.com FAQ); the programme offered "deep link in to specific markets". Exchange market URLs are public and stable (`/exchange/plus/horse-racing/market/1.xxxxxxx`), so linking a user straight into a named race market is trivial *technically* — the affiliate payment side is what closed.
- **Observed live link formats (28 Aug 2026, via myracing)**: bet365 uses `affiliate=365_XXXXXXXX` query tokens on hub landing pages, including a racing-specific funnel (`/hub/aff/open-account-horse-racing`); William Hill uses classic `btag`/`affid`/`siteid` parameters on campaigns.williamhill.com; Tote and kwiff use RavenTrack-style `trk.<domain>/o/<token>` redirects with `s1`/`s2` sub-id parameters (sub-IDs let you tag which tip/race drove the click); BoyleSports uses NetRefer `redirect.aspx?pid=&bid=`. These are **acquisition deep links** (registration funnels), not add-to-betslip links.
- **Add-to-betslip**: the verified UK example is the **Racing Post app** — "Place your bets directly from the page when you see any odds button… add all the selections to your betslip with just one click" (help.racingpost.com, article updated 5 Jul 2026). But RP achieves this via **direct B2B integrations with exactly four bookmakers (William Hill, Ladbrokes, Coral, Paddy Power)** — users log into their bookmaker account *inside* the RP app and bets go "direct with each bookmaker via a secure connection". This is a negotiated integration, not a public API.
- **Oddschecker**: its odds-grid buttons click out to bookmakers with affiliate tracking; the outbound URL construction is JavaScript-generated and **could not be inspected** this session, so whether a given bookmaker lands with the selection pre-loaded in the betslip could not be verified. FairPlay's "Connect Components" (zero-code betting widgets with "managed monetisation") is the closest thing to a UK/IE white-label for this.
- **Aggregator APIs**: **MetaBet** (metabet.io) sells exactly the desired product — "ExpressLinks… connect users directly to betting slips", automatic deep-link injection — but supports "All US Sportsbooks, in all legal states" only; **no UK/IE coverage**. No UK/Ireland equivalent offering one-click betslip API across bookmakers could be verified.
- **Practical takeaway**: for UK/IE racing, plan on (a) per-bookmaker affiliate links to race-specific landing pages where the program supplies them, (b) exchange market URLs for Matchbook/Betfair, and (c) treating true one-click add-to-betslip as a bookmaker-by-bookmaker negotiation once you have volume. Betslip-level deep-link schemes exist inside affiliate portals (creative/link generators) but are not publicly documented.

## 5. Bet tracking: how the app can record users' bets

**Betfair Exchange — the only consented read-API, and it is official.** From the Betfair Exchange API documentation (offline PDF "BetfairAPIDocsOfflineVersion(220825).pdf", dated 22 Aug 2025, via betfair-developer-docs.atlassian.net):
- `listCurrentOrders` returns the user's open bets; `listClearedOrders` "retrieve[s] details of a settled bet/market" with roll-up options — i.e. full open + settled bet history for P&L/CLV dashboards.
- **App keys**: every Betfair account gets a free **Delayed App Key** (delayed prices, no traded-volume fields — fine for development and for account/order endpoints) and can activate a **Live App Key for a one-off £299 fee** ("A one-off activation fee of £299 applies; this is debited directly from your Betfair account once access is approved"). Applications are not accepted from India, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka or the UAE.
- **Third-party access with user consent** is formally supported via the **Vendor Program** (developer.betfair.com/en/vendor-program/the-process/): the **Vendor Web API** "is available to licensed Software Vendors who are creating web-based applications… to carry out operations on the users behalf using the OAuth2 protocol" — redirect the user to `identitysso.betfair.com/view/vendor-login?client_id=<vendorId>&response_type=code&redirect_uri=…`, exchange the code for access + refresh tokens, then call the API as the user (operations: `token`, `revokeAccessToWebApp`, `isAccountSubscribedToWebApp`; users can revoke). Desktop-style apps instead use the **Vendor Services API** subscription-token flow (`getApplicationSubscriptionToken` → user interactive login → `activateApplicationSubscription`). Web vendor apps are not permitted for betfair.it; Spain/Romania/Australia have separate SSO endpoints. This is precisely the "read the user's own placed bets with their consent" mechanism — but note it requires Betfair vendor registration/licensing, and (post-May-2025) it is decoupled from any UK/IE affiliate payment.
- **Precedents**: the entire Betfair trading-software ecosystem (Bet Angel, Geeks Toy, RacingTraders et al.) runs on these vendor flows, proving longevity of the mechanism (products in market for 15+ years).
- **Betdaq/Smarkets/Matchbook**: Betdaq has an API historically but no affiliate program; Smarkets has a REST API for trading; Matchbook offers an affiliate odds-feed API (api@matchbook.com). None of these was verified this session to offer a formal third-party *vendor/OAuth* scheme like Betfair's.

**Bookmakers — no consumer read-API.** No Irish/UK bookmaker exposes an API for third parties to read a customer's bet history. Comparable products therefore use one of:
1. **Be the betslip** (bets placed through you): Racing Post's model — in-app login to William Hill/Ladbrokes/Coral/Paddy Power, "My Bookmaker Accounts" then shows open & settled bets and balances (help.racingpost.com articles updated 2024–2026). Requires bookmaker B2B deals.
2. **Manual bet logging**: standard for tipster platforms — OLBG tracks tips (not stakes) per member; most bet-tracker apps in UK/IE are manual-entry spreadsheets-with-UX. (Specific app-by-app audit was out of reach this session; no UK/IE product was found that auto-syncs bookmaker bets. **Could not verify** any exception.)
3. **Exchange-native tracking**: via the Betfair vendor flow above — automatic and consented, but only for exchange bets.

**Implication for the app**: the realistic v1 is manual logging plus optional "Connect Betfair" (vendor OAuth, delayed key during development, £299 live key at launch) for automatic exchange P&L — mirroring what the trading-tool ecosystem already does. Automatic bookmaker sync should be assumed unavailable.

## 6. Realistic revenue math (what can actually be sourced)

Published per-player or EPC figures for UK/Irish **racing** affiliates specifically **could not be verified** — none of the audited programs publishes a rate card, and affiliate-industry EPC surveys accessible this session did not break out UK/IE racing. Verifiable anchors:

- **Entain Partners**: "€6+ million every month" paid across "10,000+" affiliates → a **mean ≈ €600/affiliate/month**, with a power-law distribution (the median affiliate certainly earns far less). Source: entainpartners.com (fetched 28 Aug 2026).
- **Revshare band**: 10–40% of net revenue (Punter2Pro, upd. 21 Jul 2026); verified fixed points: BoyleSports **30%** sports NGR; Entain "up to 35%".
- **What one referred recreational racing punter is worth**: at 30% revshare, a punter losing £300/yr nets the affiliate ~£90/yr (before dead months and negative carryover); a *winning* value-bettor is worth £0 under bookmaker revshare, negative under carryover — and under an exchange deal (Matchbook), a winning £2,000-turnover-per-month exchange user paying ~2% commission on winnings might generate only a few euros/month of shareable commission. This asymmetry, not the rates, is the economics headline.
- **Channel-scale datapoints**: Flutter sold Oddschecker for **£155m** (Jul 2021); 888affiliates claims **$207,905,405** paid to affiliates lifetime (affiliates.888.com, fetched 28 Aug 2026); OLBG has 500k lifetime members / 1.1m app installs monetised almost entirely by operator commissions; StatsDrone (affiliate-dashboard aggregator) tracks **2,174 iGaming affiliate programs**, indicating how commoditised the tooling is.
- **Quota math for a small site**: to merely *keep* a bet365 account you need 15 active customers per rolling 3 months; to keep full Tote terms, ~10 new customers/month; BoyleSports needs 1+/month. A niche subscription app funnelling, say, 100 free-tier users to sign-ups in year one will struggle to hold more than 2–3 bookmaker accounts at healthy status simultaneously — concentrate the funnel.

## 7. Ireland-specific notes (GRAI)

Legal analysis was covered by other researchers; the affiliate-relevant deltas found here:
- **Program documents lag GRAI**: Entain's T&Cs (Dec 2024) contain country appendices for the UK, Italy, Spain etc. but *no Ireland appendix and no mention of Ireland*; BoyleSports' T&Cs date from May 2022. Expect re-papering as GRAI remote-licensing beds in (regime open for applications since ~Feb 2026 — "Ireland's new gambling licensing regime now open for applications", Yogonet, 5 Feb 2026); until then, contractual Irish-market compliance obligations are unclear in several programs — **flag as stale/moving**.
- **Tote Affiliates already hard-codes Irish compliance**: marketing to Irish customers must carry Irish problem-gambling wording, and traffic is contractually restricted to UK+IE.
- **FDJ United (Unibet) does not list Ireland** among its licensed jurisdictions on the affiliate site — verify before sending Irish traffic.
- Market flux: **Betfred paused Irish operations ahead of the new GRAI regime** (World Casino News, 19 Jun 2026) — the GRAI-licensed operator set (and hence the legally promotable set for an Irish affiliate) is still settling.
