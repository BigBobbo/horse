# Betting-Edge Apps & Services Outside UK/Ireland Horse Racing — Business-Model and Feature Audit

*Research date: 2026-08-28. Focus: what a daily-suggestions horse-racing app for Ireland can copy from (1) US racing products, (2) Australia/Hong Kong racing products, and (3) sport-agnostic value/arb/odds tooling. All prices verified against primary sources where possible; staleness flagged inline.*

---

## Key takeaways

- **Three distinct monetisation layers recur everywhere:** raw/enriched **data** (per-card or subscription), **model output** (ratings/rated prices/win probabilities), and **picks/tickets** (human or AI selections). The most successful products sell the *middle* layer — model output framed as "your edge" — and give away or subsidise the outer layers. An Irish app should launch at the model-output layer with a picks veneer on top.
- **Per-race-card micro-pricing is uniquely American** (DRF Formulator $5.50/card, Brisnet Ultimate PPs ~$3/card, Equibase Race Lens $4/card + $10 day pass) and reflects the "handicapper buys the card for today's track" culture. Day passes and weekend passes (EquinEdge $9.99/day, $25 weekend — 2021 figures) are a proven low-commitment on-ramp worth copying for festival days (Cheltenham, Punchestown, Galway).
- **"Free with wager" is the dominant US distribution hack:** TwinSpires gives Brisnet PPs and picks free if you bet the race through them; DRF gives a free Formulator card per $50 wagered on DRF Bets. The data product becomes a customer-acquisition cost for the wagering business — a model an Irish app could replicate via bookmaker affiliate deals.
- **Betfair Australia's Hub proves "free model as funnel" works:** free daily rated prices for nearly every AU thoroughbred and greyhound meeting (built on licensed Punting Form data), open-source GitHub tutorials, and datathons ($5,000 greyhound 2025; $50,000 thoroughbred 2023) — all to convert quants into exchange customers. Betfair operates the same exchange in Ireland; this is directly relevant infrastructure and marketing precedent.
- **Punting Form is the best single template for an Irish product:** Free → Starter A$59/mo (interactive form + CSV/JSON API) → Professional A$297/mo (200m sectionals, benchmarks) → Modeller (custom-priced point-in-time historical DB with Betfair prices). It was acquired by BetMakers in Oct 2022 — proof of exit value for a national-scale racing data/ratings business.
- **Value-betting SaaS pricing clusters at €/$99–€400/mo** (RebelBetting $99–209/mo, Trademate €120–400/mo, BetBurger €79.99–319.99/mo, OddsJam ~$59–$198+/mo), justified by "the tool pays for itself" framing, profit guarantees (RebelBetting: free extra months until you profit), and CLV (closing-line value) tracking as the proof-of-edge metric.
- **Trust infrastructure is a product in itself:** Pyckio/Tipstrr (verified tipster marketplaces, ~50% revenue share to tipsters, Tipstrr acquired Pyckio Nov 2024) and Smart Betting Club (£139.99+VAT/yr independent tipster auditing) monetise *verification*. Any picks product for Ireland must publish a full, timestamped, odds-referenced bet log from day one — it is both marketing and moat.
- **Hong Kong is the proof that model + odds-feedback beats racing markets:** Bill Benter's syndicate won close to US$1bn betting HKJC pools with a ~100+ variable logit model, with the decisive step being feeding **live public odds back into the model**. Lesson for the app: the "perceived value vs available odds" component isn't a bolt-on — blending model probability with market probability is where most of the predictive power comes from.

## Cluster 1 — US horse racing products

### DRF (Daily Racing Form) — Formulator, Classic PPs, TimeformUS
- **What it is:** The incumbent US past-performance (PP) publisher. Formulator is its interactive PP software (filterable running lines, trainer patterns, custom speed-figure views); Classic PPs are static PDFs; TimeformUS (acquired US arm of Timeform) is a modernised PP set with proprietary speed figures and a graphical **Pace Projector** that visualises the projected running shape of the race.
- **Pricing (verified on shop.drf.com, Aug 2026):** per-card: Classic $4.95, TimeformUS $4.25, Formulator $5.50, and a bundled "All Access PPs" (all three views, switchable) at $6.35/card ([shop.drf.com/compare-pp-plans](https://shop.drf.com/compare-pp-plans)). DRF's help pages cite a base Formulator monthly 5-card plan at $21.25 and an unlimited monthly at ~$239 ([help.drf.com](https://help.drf.com/hc/en-us/articles/225684848-How-much-does-it-cost)) — the help-page figures may be slightly stale but match the shop's "save with plans" structure. TimeformUS standalone subs were folded into DRF.com; historic standalone pricing was ~$74.95/mo unlimited ([timeformusblog.com](https://timeformusblog.com/2022/04/25/get-the-2022-timeformus-kentucky-derby-package-now/), [timeformus.com/products](https://timeformus.com/products)).
- **Distribution hack:** free Formulator card per $50 wagered through DRF Bets ([drf.com](https://www.drf.com/news/drf-offering-free-formulator-drf-bets-customers)).
- **Lesson:** (a) tiered *views* of the same data (basic PDF → interactive tool → premium figures) at stepped prices; (b) the "All Access" bundle shows users will pay a premium (+15–50%) to switch presentation lenses on one race; (c) big-event packages (Kentucky Derby bundles) are reliable spike revenue — Irish equivalent: Cheltenham/Galway packs.

### Brisnet / TwinSpires (Churchill Downs)
- **What it is:** Brisnet sells PP products (Quick Play, Premium Plus, **Ultimate PPs** with Prime Power rating, pace/speed figures, pedigree and trainer stats); TwinSpires is Churchill Downs' ADW (betting app) that distributes them.
- **Pricing:** Ultimate PPs historically $3.00/card, with member-tier discounts to $1.50/$1.00 ([brisnet.com price list PDF](https://www.brisnet.com/library/productpricelist.pdf); the $3 figure dates to 2012 per [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/horse-racing/story/_/id/7608372/brisnet-offers-ultimate-performances) — flag: current per-card price not re-verified, brisnet.com timed out during this audit). **All Brisnet Thoroughbred PDF PPs and Insider Picks are free-with-bet on TwinSpires** — download the product, wager on that track/date, and the charge is waived ([support.twinspires.com](https://support.twinspires.com/hc/en-us/articles/22002824471949-What-Products-are-free-with-bet)).
- **Lesson:** the cleanest expression of "data as CAC for wagering". An Irish app that earns bookmaker/exchange affiliate revenue could rebate subscription cost against tracked bet volume.

### Equibase / TrackMaster / STATS Race Lens
- **What it is:** Equibase is the US industry-owned official data collector (the jurisdiction-level monopoly — an instructive contrast to Ireland, where HRI/RÁS data flows through commercial licensees). It retails PPs and premium tools; TrackMaster (its brand) sells ratings and the RDSS "decision modelling" tool that exports to Excel with a live tote connection ([equibase.com/premium/eqptrackmasterrdss.cfm](https://www.equibase.com/premium/eqptrackmasterrdss.cfm)). **Race Lens** (built with STATS) is a query engine over historical races — angle-finding as a product.
- **Pricing:** Race Lens: $4.00 single card, $10.00 unlimited day pass, subscriptions "as low as $17.50" ([equibase.com Race Lens pages](https://www.equibase.com/premium/eqbProductPage.cfm?pid=50610)). Equibase also sells an all-products race-day bundle (9 products, single price) ([equibase.com](https://www.equibase.com/premium/eqpEquibasefullpp.cfm)) and gives away a free registration "handicappers' dataset" ([equibase.com/handicappersdata.cfm](https://www.equibase.com/handicappersdata.cfm)). No public developer API — data access is via licensed partnerships only; third parties fill the gap with scrapers (e.g. Apify Equibase scraper) ([apify.com](https://apify.com/jungle_synthesizer/equibase-us-horse-racing-scraper/api)).
- **Lesson:** (a) the **day pass** ($10, all products, one race day) is a superb casual-user SKU; (b) a *query/angle-builder* UI (Race Lens, and betmix Angler below) is a differentiated feature beyond static ratings — "find me trainers 20%+ with first-time blinkers at Dundalk" is a compelling Irish feature.

### EquinEdge — the closest existing analogue to the user's app idea
- **What it is:** AI handicapping platform founded by handicapper Scotty McKeever. Outputs per-horse **EE Win %** (model win probability; top pick wins 32.9% of races per its marketing), proprietary Pace metric (top pace figure is 1st/2nd at the first call 72.5% of the time), **GSR (Genetic Strength Rating)** for surface/distance suitability from relatives' records, Strength-of-Race, condensed "HandiView" PPs, and a **Ticket Generator** that turns model output into actual wager constructions ([equinedge.com](https://equinedge.com/)).
- **Pricing:** 2021 reporting: $9.99 day pass, $25 Fri–Sun pass, $50/mo weekend-only, $80/mo unlimited ([CDC Gaming](https://cdcgaming.com/focus-on-equinedge-artificial-intelligence-equips-the-next-generation-of-horse-players/)). **Flag: current 2026 plan prices could not be verified** (pricing page is behind a signup wall returning 403); treat exact figures as stale, structure as accurate. Also distributes **B2B2C**: tracks and racinos get the product free and offer it to their customers as a perk ([CDC Gaming](https://cdcgaming.com/focus-on-equinedge-artificial-intelligence-equips-the-next-generation-of-horse-players/)).
- **Marketing:** CEO won the 2023 Pegasus World Cup Betting Challenge (~$200k) using the product — contest wins as credibility ([CDC Gaming](https://cdcgaming.com/equinedge-showcases-horse-racing-platform-that-won-betting-contest/)). Community layer: 7,000+ users, live web shows, coaching, private Facebook VIP group with ticket shares.
- **Lesson:** this is the feature blueprint for the user's app: model win% per horse → value flag vs odds → ticket construction → community/education wrapper. Note EquinEdge sells *probabilities and tickets*, not raw data, and prices at a mass-market $50–80/mo rather than pro-tool $300/mo.

### In The Money Media (ITM)
- **What it is:** Podcast-network-first media business (Players' Podcast is US racing's longest-running podcast) with a premium layer: **ITM Plus** at **$20/mo or $199/yr** — private podcast feed, show notes, pro-horseplayer interviews, "Highlight Horses, Proprietary Information and Profitable Angles"; an "Inner Circle" tier adds interactive livestreams and **bet shares** ([inthemoney.substack.com/about](https://inthemoney.substack.com/about), [inthemoneypodcast.com](https://inthemoneypodcast.com/)).
- **Lesson:** content-first funnel — free daily audio builds parasocial trust, the paid tier sells *proximity to the experts*, and bet-shares (watching a pro construct real tickets) is the conversion feature. Cheap to bolt onto an app (weekly "how the model sees Leopardstown" audio/notes).

### betmix
- **What it is:** DIY model-builder: users weight ~100+ handicapping factors into their own rating ("mix"), backtest it on the historical database, and the **Angler** module data-mines conditions (trainer/jockey/betting patterns) to auto-find profitable angles ([betmix.com](https://betmix.com/), [betmix.com/tools](https://betmix.com/tools/)).
- **Pricing:** $50/mo unlimited (marketed as "$1.67/day"), free race of the day, free Angler sandbox page ([news.1st.com/betmix-pricing](https://news.1st.com/betmix-pricing), [betmix.com/free-race-of-the-day](https://betmix.com/free-race-of-the-day/)).
- **Lesson:** "build your own model" converts sceptics who don't trust black boxes; a free daily race is a proven trial mechanic. Consider exposing model-factor weights as user-tunable presets in the Irish app.

### EQB Inc / AmWest Entertainment
- **EQB (Jeff Seder):** *not* a bettor product — biomechanics/bloodstock analytics (cardiac ultrasound, gait analysis, proprietary databases built over 40 years) sold to owners for yearling selection; famously advised buying American Pharoah ([eqb.fyi](https://www.eqb.fyi/), [Meb Faber podcast](https://mebfaber.com/2021/04/28/e306-jeff-seder/)). Lesson: proprietary *physical* data creates a defensible niche, but it's a B2B bloodstock business, not relevant to a daily betting app beyond illustrating that unique data > better math on shared data.
- **AmWest Entertainment:** operator of the **AmWager** ADW — differentiates a *betting platform* on handicapper-grade tooling: Pro% factor-weighting, ABC ticket structuring, conditional wagering, real-time dutching, free video from 200+ tracks ([amwestentertainment.com](https://www.amwestentertainment.com/), [BloodHorse](https://www.bloodhorse.com/horse-racing/articles/225005/amwager-introduces-abc-wagering-new-features)). Lesson: wager-construction UX (dutching calculators, ABC tickets, conditional bets) is a differentiator even when odds/data are commoditised.

## Cluster 2 — Australia / Hong Kong

### Punting Form (BetMakers) — the template
- **What it is:** Australian form/sectional-times data house: interactive form guides, speed maps, ratings, 200m sectional benchmarks, and an API. Founded 2015, **acquired by BetMakers Oct 2022** ([CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/punting-form)). Customers include pro punters, syndicates, bookmakers, trainer Ciaron Maher, and Racing.com ([puntingform.com.au](https://www.puntingform.com.au/)).
- **Pricing (verified on puntingform.com.au/pricing, Aug 2026):** **Free** (form guides, speed maps, free Saturday-metro ratings) → **Starter A$59/mo** (interactive form, CSV/JSON API) → **Professional A$297/mo** (raw 200m sectionals for AU/HK/SG, benchmark data, race-speed assessments) → **Modeller, custom-priced** (point-in-time historical DB incl. Betfair prices, personal use) ([puntingform.com.au/pricing](https://www.puntingform.com.au/pricing)). Also sells **PF AI Ratings** to Starter+ subscribers with published performance vs TAB fixed odds ([docs.puntingform.com.au](https://docs.puntingform.com.au/docs/punting-form-ai-ratings)).
- **Lesson:** the four-tier ladder (free ratings → cheap API → expensive sectionals → bespoke modeller DB) monetises everyone from casual punter to syndicate, and "point-in-time database with exchange prices" is precisely the product a serious Irish modeller would pay for. Publishing the AI ratings' P&L against fixed odds is the credibility move.

### Ratings2Win
- **What it is:** Pro-grade ratings/database software (**Axis**) for Australia and Hong Kong: performance & sectional ratings, wide/lanes data, margins-from-leader, track-bias analysis, adjustable speed maps; 730 days of history standard, extensions back to 2010 as paid add-ons ([ratings2win.com.au](https://ratings2win.com.au/), [FAQ](https://ratings2win.com.au/faq/)). Claims 28% top-pick strike rate / 60.6% top-3 across 33,000+ races (2017–19).
- **Pricing:** **not published** — contact-sales only (verified on FAQ). Separate AU and HK subscriptions plus combined bundles.
- **Lesson:** opaque contact-pricing signals a high-ticket pro niche; also note both AU services treat **Hong Kong as an add-on market** — small, data-rich jurisdictions (like Ireland) can be packaged as premium verticals of a bigger product.

### DynamicOdds
- **What it is:** Australia's leading racing odds-comparison/trading screen since 2009: live odds from 35+ bookmakers + Betfair, price-flucs charts, market percentages, alerts (screen/audio/email), speed maps, and **multi-bookmaker bet placement from one interface**; an authorised Betfair app ([dynamicodds.com](https://dynamicodds.com/), [apps.betfair.com](https://apps.betfair.com/arbitrage/dynamicodds-premium/)). Its terms are hosted on a BetMakers domain — it sits in the BetMakers group ([dolanding.betmakers.com/terms-and-conditions](https://dolanding.betmakers.com/terms-and-conditions)).
- **Pricing:** **A$35 per 4 weeks**, 14-day free trial ([dynamicodds.com](https://dynamicodds.com/)).
- **Lesson:** at ~€21/mo it shows odds-screen + bet-routing can be a cheap mass product; "price alert when your horse hits your target odds" is a low-cost, high-retention feature for a value-betting app.

### Betfair Australia Hub — free models, education, datathons
- **What it is:** Betfair AU publishes **free daily rated prices** for nearly every AU thoroughbred meeting (model built on licensed Punting Form data, converts win% to rated price, highlights a "Value %" vs market) and a greyhound model built on state racing-body data ([betfair.com.au horse model](https://www.betfair.com.au/hub/racing/horse-racing/predictions-model/), [greyhound model](https://www.betfair.com.au/hub/racing/greyhound-tips/greyhound-predictions-model/)). The **Automation Hub** (betfair-datascientists.github.io + GitHub org) offers open-source Python/R tutorials for building racing models and automating via the Betfair API ([betfair-datascientists.github.io](https://betfair-datascientists.github.io/), [github.com/betfair-datascientists](https://github.com/betfair-datascientists)). Regular **datathons**: $5,000 prize greyhound datathon (May 2025), $50,000 thoroughbred datathon on Punting Form data (2023) ([betfair-datascientists.github.io/modelling/greyhoundDatathon](https://betfair-datascientists.github.io/modelling/greyhoundDatathon/), [Racing and Sports](https://www.racingandsports.com.au/news/racing/other/2023-10-05/betfair-hub-show-off-your-data-modelling-skills-this-spring/628509)).
- **Why it matters for Ireland:** Betfair's exchange covers Irish/UK racing with deep liquidity; the AU Hub's playbook (free rated prices as content, tutorials to grow API users, datathons for community) is proof that an exchange-adjacent model product grows the ecosystem rather than cannibalising it. The user's app can position as exactly this for Irish racing — and Betfair AU shows the *bookmaker pays for the model content* variant is viable too (sponsorship/affiliate angle).

### TopSport (market-maker angle)
- **What it is:** Family-owned Australian bookmaker (Merlehan family, 35+ years) famous for **welcoming winning punters**, best-tote/best-of-best racing products, low minimums "no maximums", NSW Bookmakers' Co-operative payment guarantee ([newbettingsites.com.au review](https://www.newbettingsites.com.au/en/reviews/topsport/), [mybettingsites.com/au/topsport](https://mybettingsites.com/au/topsport)).
- **Lesson:** in AU a genuine market-maker culture survives; in Ireland/UK, soft bookmakers restrict winners quickly. A value-betting app must plan for account limiting: route users toward the exchange (Betfair), tote, and winner-friendly books, and set expectations — this is a core product-design constraint, not an afterthought.

### Hong Kong Jockey Club — data culture and quant success
- **The Benter story:** Bill Benter's Hong Kong syndicate, building on Bolton & Chapman's multinomial-logit academic work, grew to a ~100+ variable model and won **close to US$1 billion**; the breakthrough came when he **fed the HKJC's live public odds into the model** and bet only where model and market diverged; the 1990–91 season alone yielded ~$3m, later single seasons $50m+ ([Bloomberg, 2018](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2018-05-03/the-gambler-who-cracked-the-horse-racing-code), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/magazines/post-magazine/long-reads/article/2150721/how-american-gambler-unlocked-secret-hong-kong), [Guinness World Records, Aug 2025](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2025/8/a-billion-dollars-off-the-ponies-how-a-statistician-became-the-most-profitable-gambler)).
- **Why HK worked:** enormous parimutuel pools concentrated on only two tracks (~8 races a meeting, ~1,200 horses total population), complete and consistent official data — HKJC publishes rich free data incl. sectional times, and is repeatedly cited as best-in-class for free racing data ([Hacker News discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=20866388)); community datasets scrape full sectionals back to 2008 ([github.com/eprochasson/horserace_data](https://github.com/eprochasson/horserace_data)), and commercial scrapers sell "syndicate-grade" HKJC feeds ([apify.com](https://apify.com/alaricus/hkjc-comprehensive-racing-data)).
- **Lesson:** (a) closed, small, well-measured populations are where models win — Irish racing (small horse population, centralised HRI data) resembles HK more than the sprawling US scene; (b) the **market-blend** insight (model probability × market probability) is the single most important technical takeaway for the user's "value vs odds" component; (c) parimutuel/tote pools don't ban winners — the Tote in Ireland plus exchange are the ban-proof outlets.

## Cluster 3 — Sport-agnostic value/arb/odds tools (business models to copy)

### RebelBetting (Sweden)
- Value betting + sure betting (arbitrage) SaaS. **Pricing (verified rebelbetting.com/pricing):** Starter $99/mo, Pro $209/mo; annual = Starter $69/mo, Pro $139/mo (30% off); 14-day free trial, no card; trial capped at 50 bets/day ([rebelbetting.com/pricing](https://www.rebelbetting.com/pricing)). **Profit guarantee:** "if you don't make a profit the first month, you get another month free — again and again until you profit." Publishes monthly aggregated customer-profit reports (e.g. "over a quarter million euros", Oct 2025) ([rebelbetting.com](https://www.rebelbetting.com/customer-results/value-betting-results-october-2025)).
- **Lessons:** the profit guarantee reverses risk perception and is cheap to honour (churned losers rarely claim); publishing aggregate user P&L is the strongest possible social proof for a "we find you value" product.

### Trademate Sports (Norway)
- Value-betting tool centred on **CLV (closing line value)** as the north-star metric, with Kelly-based staking, edge tracking, and portfolio analytics. **Pricing:** Core €120/mo (or €300/3mo) targeting 90+ soft bookmakers; Pro €400/mo (or €1,000/3mo) for Asian books/exchanges ([punter2pro.com](https://punter2pro.com/product/trademate-sports-core-pro-subscription/), [thearbacademy.com](https://thearbacademy.com/trademate-sports-review/)); 30-day value guarantee ([tradematesports.com/en/value-guarantee](https://www.tradematesports.com/en/value-guarantee)).
- **Lessons:** CLV tracking is directly portable to racing (did the model's advised price beat the SP/BSP?) — build "did we beat the close?" into the app's feedback loop; segmenting soft-book vs sharp-market users into different price tiers doubles ARPU on pros.

### OddsJam / OpticOdds (US)
- Odds-comparison + positive-EV + arb + promo-conversion toolkit across 100+ US sportsbooks; EV method = compare each book's line to sharp-consensus fair value. **Consumer pricing:** RotoWire's 2026 review lists ~$59/mo (Fantasy), $198/mo Gold ($162/mo annual), Platinum custom with live-betting tools ([rotowire.com](https://www.rotowire.com/betting/oddsjam-review)); other roundups cite $99–$499/mo across packages ([oddspapi.io](https://oddspapi.io/blog/oddsjam-api-alternative/)). **B2B:** the developer API is a separate custom-priced product, **OpticOdds** ([oddspapi.io](https://oddspapi.io/blog/oddsjam-api-alternative/)). OddsJam/OpticOdds was acquired by Gambling.com Group (2025) — flag: acquisition detail from general knowledge, not re-verified in this audit.
- **Lessons:** one odds-infrastructure investment monetised twice (consumer SaaS + B2B API); "deposit-match promo converter" tools show that *teaching users to extract bookmaker promos* is itself a paid feature — very relevant to the offer-dense Irish market.

### BetBurger (arb/value scanner)
- **Pricing (verified betburger.com/prices):** Prematch €79.99/mo, Live €279.99/mo, Prematch+Live €319.99/mo; granular durations from 1 day (€5.99) to 360 days (~€1.94/day); 600+ bookmakers, 40 sports; surebets + valuebets + middles, Telegram bot delivery ([betburger.com/prices](https://betburger.com/prices)).
- **Lessons:** 1-day and 7-day SKUs monetise the curious; Telegram-bot delivery of opportunities is cheap to build and matches how Irish punters already consume tips.

### Oddschecker (odds comparison / affiliate)
- Free-to-user odds grid for UK/IE racing and sport; revenue = affiliate commissions from bookmakers; bet365 has held top grid position since 2006 and takes ~28% of betslip clicks; Flutter sold Oddschecker to Bruin Capital for up to **£155m (€181.4m)** in 2021 ([igamingcapital.mt](https://igamingcapital.mt/flutter-entertainment-agrees-to-sell-oddschecker-affiliate-for-e181-4-million/), [igbaffiliate.com](https://www.igbaffiliate.com/en/articles/sponsorships/oddschecker-expands-bet365-partnership/)).
- **Lessons:** affiliate click-throughs on "best price for this horse" can be the Irish app's entire free-tier monetisation; the nine-figure exit shows how valuable the aggregation position is.

### Exchange trading tools: Bet Angel, Geeks Toy, BetTrader; Smarkets
- **Bet Angel:** Trader edition £6/mo or £60/yr; Professional ~£149.99/yr (~£12.50/mo) with automation, Soccer Mystic/Tennis Trader, Total Performance Data integration ([betangel.com/buy/trader](https://www.betangel.com/buy/trader/), [caanberry.com review](https://caanberry.com/bet-angel-review-betfair-software/)). **Geeks Toy:** £15/mo or £150/yr, free on Betdaq/Matchbook, 14-day Betfair trial ([geekstoy.com](https://geekstoy.com/en), [betfairsquare.com](https://betfairsquare.com/software/best-betfair-trading-software)). **BetTrader (RacingTraders):** £9.99/mo or £99/yr, multi-market grid showing up to 16 races ([apps.betfair.com/trading/bettrader](https://apps.betfair.com/trading/bettrader/)).
- **Smarkets:** 2% standard commission; **1% Pro tier** mandatory opt-in above 1,500 bets or £1m staked/month; **3% "Select" tier imposed on users with >£25k net profit over 12 months**; modern REST + streaming API ([help.smarkets.com](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-us/articles/212654665-Smarkets-commission-FAQ), [caanberry.com](https://caanberry.com/smarkets-review-commission/)).
- **Lessons:** exchange tool pricing is commodity-cheap (£6–15/mo) because Betfair's £299 one-off API charge era conditioned the market — don't expect to charge SaaS-premium for *execution* features; charge for *intelligence*. Smarkets' winner-surcharge shows even exchanges now tax sharp users — another argument to route serious volume via Betfair (flat commission) and brokers.

### Mollybet and the broker model
- Betting brokers aggregate sharp books/exchanges behind one wallet: Mollybet's screen shows best available price across PS3838 (Pinnacle white-label), BETISN, Singbet, SBOBet, 18bet, 3et, Betfair, Betdaq, and splits a large stake across books automatically; **free for punters** — the broker earns from bookmaker commission baked into the flow ([bookie.broker/mollybet](https://bookie.broker/mollybet/), [betmetricslab.com](https://betmetricslab.com/betting-sites/sports-bet-brokers-agents/)). Horse-racing coverage via brokers is thin vs football, and access is often restricted/KYC-heavy for retail EU users — flag: Irish retail availability not verified.
- **Lessons:** "one wallet, best price, auto-split execution" is the end-state UX the app should imitate at whatever scale regulation allows (deep-link bet slips to multiple books at minimum).

### Tipster verification: Pyckio, Tipstrr, Smart Betting Club

## Synthesis — business-model patterns to copy for an Ireland-first daily-suggestions app

1. **Ladder the offer like Punting Form:** Free daily "race of the day" with full model output (betmix/Betfair Hub trick) → ~€15–25/mo consumer tier (daily rated prices for all IRE meetings, value flags vs live odds, price alerts) → ~€60–100/mo serious tier (full model probabilities, CLV tracking, ticket/dutching builder, API/CSV) → custom "modeller" tier (historical point-in-time DB) if the data licensing allows.
2. **Sell probabilities and tickets, not PDFs.** EquinEdge's structure (win% + value price + ticket generator + community) is the modern product; US-style per-card PPs are a legacy format.
3. **Event day-passes:** $10-day-pass economics (Race Lens/EquinEdge) mapped to Cheltenham/Punchestown/Galway weeks will out-convert monthly subs for casual Irish punters.
4. **Make the value loop honest:** blend model probability with market odds (the Benter lesson), track advised-price-vs-BSP (the Trademate CLV lesson), publish aggregate results monthly (the RebelBetting lesson), and consider a profit-guarantee-style offer.
5. **Monetise the free tier via affiliation** (Oddschecker lesson): best-odds grid per runner with affiliate links; free-with-bet rebates if a bookmaker partnership can be struck (TwinSpires/DRF lesson).
6. **Plan for restrictions:** soft Irish/UK books limit winners; design first-class flows to Betfair exchange and the Tote, and be transparent about it (TopSport/Smarkets lesson).
7. **Build community + content:** podcast/notes layer at ~€20/mo (ITM lesson), Telegram delivery of daily suggestions (BetBurger lesson), datathon/open-tutorial goodwill if courting modellers (Betfair Hub lesson).
8. **Get externally verified early:** run the model as a tipster on Tipstrr and aim for an SBC review — cheap, independent credibility.
